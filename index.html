Common questions

Do my students need to install an app?

No. They open your link in the browser on any phone or computer. They can also add it to their home screen, and it opens like an app.

Can I give questions in Hindi?

Yes. Questions, options and solutions can be in Hindi, English or both together. Images and data-interpretation sets are supported too.

What happens if a student’s internet goes off during an exam?

Answers are saved as they go. When the student opens the exam again, it continues from the same question with the remaining time.

Can students cheat by switching apps?

The exam runs in full-screen and records every tab or app switch. You see the count against each student in the live view and in the result.

How many live exams can I run?

4 live exams every month on every plan — about one every week. Practice, mock tests, previous year papers and the daily challenge have no limit.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp for a free demo. We will show you both the student and the admin side.

What if I have more than 500 students?

Talk to us on WhatsApp — we will suggest a plan for your size.
